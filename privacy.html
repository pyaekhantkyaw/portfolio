SITE INFORMATION

Privacy.

This portfolio is hosted as a static website. This page describes its optional analytics and contact features.

Visitor analytics

The site may use Cloudflare Web Analytics when enabled by the site owner. It helps measure aggregate visits, page views and performance. The portfolio does not attempt to identify individual recruiters, and it does not use its own visitor database or advertising trackers. Cloudflare may process technical data according to its policies.

Cloudflare privacy policy ↗

Contact and meeting requests

The request form prepares an email or, if configured, opens a public Airtable form. Sending an email or submitting the external form is your choice; the portfolio itself does not store contact submissions. Meeting requests require confirmation by the owner.

Airtable privacy policy ↗

Local preferences

Your browser may store your light or dark theme selection locally. No personal details are stored by the portfolio in local storage.

External links

GitHub, LinkedIn, certificate verification pages and other linked services have their own privacy policies. Contact the portfolio owner with questions.
